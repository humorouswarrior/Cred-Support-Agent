title: KYC document requirements
---

Every Cred applicant must complete KYC with one proof of identity, one proof of address
and one recent photograph before any account or loan is activated. PAN is mandatory for
all credit products, and Aadhaar-based verification is offered as the default digital
route with offline XML or a physical form as alternatives. Salaried applicants submit
three months of payslips and six months of bank statements, while self-employed
applicants submit two years of income tax returns with computation sheets. Documents
that are more than 90 days old at submission are rejected and must be re-uploaded.

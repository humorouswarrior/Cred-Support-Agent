title: Credit card fee structure
---

Cred credit cards carry a joining fee of INR 500 and an annual fee of INR 999, and the
annual fee is waived when the cardholder spends INR 2,00,000 or more in the preceding
membership year. Cash withdrawals attract a 2.5 percent fee with a floor of INR 300 per
transaction, charged on the withdrawn amount. Late payment charges are banded by
outstanding balance and run from INR 200 on balances under INR 5,000 up to INR 1,200 on
balances above INR 50,000. Overlimit usage is blocked by default and can only be enabled
by the cardholder through an explicit consent request.

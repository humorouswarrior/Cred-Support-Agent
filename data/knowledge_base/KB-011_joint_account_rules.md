title: Joint account and co-applicant rules
---

A Cred joint account may be held by up to four holders, each of whom must complete KYC
independently before the account is activated. The operating mandate is chosen at
opening as either-or-survivor, jointly, or former-or-survivor, and a change of mandate
needs the signed consent of every living holder. On a joint loan all co-applicants are
jointly and severally liable for the full outstanding amount, and a default is reported
against every holder's bureau record. Removing a holder requires closing the account and
opening a new one rather than an in-place amendment.

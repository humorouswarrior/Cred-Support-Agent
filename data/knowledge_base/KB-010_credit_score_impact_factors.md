title: What moves a credit score
---

Repayment history is the single largest driver of a bureau score and a payment more than
30 days late is reported and can pull the score down by 40 to 80 points. Credit
utilisation is the next largest factor, and sustained usage above 30 percent of the
sanctioned limit depresses the score even when every bill is paid in full. The age of
the oldest active account, the mix of secured and unsecured borrowing, and the number of
hard enquiries in the last six months make up the remainder. Checking one's own score
through Cred is a soft enquiry and never affects the score.

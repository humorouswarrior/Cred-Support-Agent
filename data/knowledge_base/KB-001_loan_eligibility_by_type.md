title: Loan eligibility by product type
---

Cred evaluates eligibility separately for each lending product rather than against a
single cut-off. A Personal Loan needs the applicant to be between 23 and 58 years old
with a verified monthly income of at least INR 25,000, while a Home Loan additionally
requires a clear property title and a loan-to-value ratio at or below 80 percent. An
Auto Loan is assessed against the on-road vehicle price and caps funding at 85 percent
for new vehicles and 70 percent for used ones. Education Loans above INR 7,50,000
require a co-applicant, and Business Loans require 24 months of filed returns for the
borrowing entity.

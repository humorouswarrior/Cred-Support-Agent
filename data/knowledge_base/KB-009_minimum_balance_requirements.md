title: Minimum balance requirements
---

Cred savings accounts carry an average monthly balance requirement of INR 10,000 in
metro and urban branches and INR 5,000 in semi-urban and rural ones. The balance is
assessed as the average of daily closing balances across the calendar month, not as a
floor on any single day. A shortfall is charged at 6 percent of the gap between the
required and maintained average, capped at INR 600 per month. Salary accounts, student
accounts and accounts held by senior citizens above 60 are exempt from the requirement
entirely.

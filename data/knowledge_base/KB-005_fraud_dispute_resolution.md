title: Fraud and transaction dispute resolution
---

A disputed or unauthorised transaction must be reported within 30 days of the statement
date for the zero-liability protection to apply. Cred acknowledges the dispute within 24
hours, issues a provisional credit within 7 working days where the claim is prima facie
valid, and completes the investigation within 45 days for domestic transactions and 90
days for cross-border ones. The card or account is placed under a fraud review hold
while the investigation runs, which pauses any linked loan disbursement. If the dispute
is decided against the customer the provisional credit is reversed with 10 days of
written notice.

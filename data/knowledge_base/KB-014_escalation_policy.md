title: Support escalation policy for pending applications
---

Cred support escalates a loan application when it is flagged for fraud review or when it
has been ageing in the queue beyond the service window for its stage. A fraud-flagged
application is routed to the financial crime desk immediately and the customer is told
only that additional verification is running. An ageing application without a fraud flag
is escalated to the underwriting supervisor once it passes the 80th percentile of
current queue age. Support agents never quote an approval probability and never promise
a disbursement date before the sanction letter is issued.

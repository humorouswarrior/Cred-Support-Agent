title: Loan application status lifecycle
---

A Cred loan application moves through five states: Submitted, Under Review, Approved or
Rejected, and finally Disbursed. Submitted means the form and documents have been
received but underwriting has not started, and Under Review means credit and
verification checks are actively running. Approved means the sanction letter has been
issued and Disbursed means funds have left the lending account. A Rejected application
can be reapplied for after 30 days with fresh documents, and reapplying sooner produces
an automatic decline.

title: Prepayment and foreclosure penalties
---

Floating-rate loans taken by an individual borrower carry no prepayment or foreclosure
charge at any point in the tenure. Fixed-rate Personal and Business Loans attract a 3
percent foreclosure charge on the outstanding principal if closed within the first 12
months, dropping to 2 percent between months 13 and 24 and to nil thereafter.
Part-prepayment is free up to 25 percent of the outstanding principal in any
twelve-month window, and amounts above that are charged at the applicable foreclosure
rate. Every prepayment is applied to principal first, and the borrower chooses whether
to shorten the tenure or reduce the EMI.

title: NRI account eligibility
---

An applicant who has been resident outside India for 182 days or more in the preceding
financial year is eligible for an NRE or NRO account with Cred. NRE accounts hold
repatriable foreign earnings and their interest is exempt from Indian income tax, while
NRO accounts hold income earned in India and their interest is taxed at source. Account
opening requires a valid passport, a valid visa or residence permit, and overseas
address proof no older than three months. Home Loans are available to NRI applicants but
require a resident Indian co-applicant or a registered power of attorney.

title: Interest rate slabs by product and credit band
---

Cred prices each product in three credit bands derived from the applicant's bureau
score. Personal Loans run from 11.5 percent for scores above 780, to 14.9 percent for
scores between 700 and 779, to 18.5 percent below 700. Home Loans are priced far tighter
at 8.4 to 9.6 percent because the exposure is secured, and Auto Loans sit between 9.2
and 13.0 percent depending on whether the vehicle is new or used. Business Loans start
at 15.0 percent and Education Loans at 10.25 percent, and all rates are floating unless
a fixed-rate option is bought at sanction.

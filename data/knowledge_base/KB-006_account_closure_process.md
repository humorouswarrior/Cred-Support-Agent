title: Account closure process
---

A closure request can be raised in the app or in writing, and Cred processes it within 7
working days once every prerequisite is satisfied. The account must carry a zero or
positive balance, no active lien, no unbilled EMI and no open dispute before closure is
permitted. Any accrued interest is paid out and the residual balance is transferred to a
verified account in the same holder's name. Closure confirmation is issued in writing,
and a closed account number is never reissued to another customer.
